PLEASE TAKE NOTICE that pursuant to CPLR Article 31 (and 22 NYCRR
§ 202.20-c for interrogatories where applicable), Plaintiff
demands that Defendants respond to the following within thirty (30)
days of service.

## DEFINITIONS AND INSTRUCTIONS
1. "You" or "Your" means Defendant(s) and any agent, employee,
   attorney, or person acting on Defendant's behalf.
2. "Document" has the broadest meaning permitted under CPLR
   § 3120 and includes ESI in native format with metadata.
3. "Communication" means any transmission of information,
   including emails, texts, chats, voicemails, and letters.
4. "Relevant Period" means [START DATE] through the present unless
   otherwise specified.
5. Identify all documents withheld on privilege via a privilege log
   per 22 NYCRR § 202.20-a.

---

## PART A — INTERROGATORIES  (CPLR 3130–3133)

1. Identify each person with knowledge of the facts alleged in
   the Complaint, stating for each: name, last known address,
   telephone, email, and a brief description of the knowledge.

2. Identify each agreement, written or oral, between Plaintiff
   and Defendant relating to [SUBJECT], stating for each: date,
   parties, subject matter, and current location of any writing.

3. State the date and amount of each payment received from or
   sent to Plaintiff during the Relevant Period, the method of
   payment, and the account from or to which it was paid.

4. Identify each insurance policy that may provide coverage for
   any claim in this action, stating: insurer, policy number,
   limits, and current status of any tender.

5. State the basis for each affirmative defense asserted in
   Defendant's Answer, identifying the documents and witnesses
   supporting it.

6. [Add matter-specific interrogatories — keep total ≤ 25 unless
   leave granted.]

## PART B — REQUESTS FOR PRODUCTION  (CPLR 3120)

1. All communications between Plaintiff and Defendant during the
   Relevant Period.

2. All documents identified or referenced in your responses to
   the Interrogatories above.

3. All contracts, leases, riders, addenda, and amendments
   between Plaintiff and Defendant.

4. All accounting records, ledgers, invoices, receipts, and bank
   statements reflecting transactions with Plaintiff during the
   Relevant Period.

5. All photographs, videos, and CCTV footage of [LOCATION] from
   [START DATE] to present, in native format with metadata.

6. All insurance policies identified in response to
   Interrogatory No. 4, including declarations pages and
   endorsements.

7. All documents reflecting any communications with third parties
   concerning Plaintiff or the events alleged in the Complaint.

8. The complete email mailbox(es) for [CUSTODIAN(S)] during the
   Relevant Period, in PST/MBOX with full headers.

9. [Add matter-specific RFPs.]
