Dear [RECIPIENT]:

You are hereby on notice that litigation is reasonably anticipated
or pending. You and your agents, employees, contractors, and IT
vendors must immediately suspend all routine destruction,
overwriting, deletion, recycling, or alteration of any documents,
data, or electronically stored information ("ESI") that may be
relevant to the matter referenced above.

## I. Scope — Subjects to Preserve  (non-exhaustive)
1. All communications between [PARTY A] and [PARTY B] from
   [START DATE] to present, including:
     - email (sent, received, drafts, deleted, archives);
     - SMS / iMessage / WhatsApp / Signal / Telegram;
     - voicemails and call logs;
     - chat platforms (Slack, Teams, etc.).
2. All contracts, leases, addenda, riders, side letters,
   work orders, invoices, receipts, ledgers, and accounting
   records relating to [SUBJECT].
3. All photographs, videos, and CCTV footage capturing
   [LOCATION / SUBJECT] from [START DATE] forward.
4. All metadata associated with the above (creation dates,
   modification history, sender/recipient headers, geolocation).
5. All backups and archival copies, including offsite or cloud
   backups (Google Workspace, Microsoft 365, Dropbox, iCloud,
   AWS, etc.).
6. [Add matter-specific items.]

## II. Custodians  (non-exhaustive)
- [NAME / ROLE]
- [NAME / ROLE]
- IT administrator / MSP currently servicing your systems

## III. Specific Affirmative Steps Required
a. Suspend any auto-delete, retention, or rotation policies that
   would purge items within the scope above.
b. Disable any "ephemeral message" features for in-scope
   custodians (Signal disappearing messages, etc.).
c. Image or otherwise capture mobile devices used by in-scope
   custodians.
d. Notify each custodian in writing of this hold and retain a
   copy of that notice.
e. Preserve cloud account contents even if a custodian leaves
   the organization.

## IV. Spoliation Warning
Failure to preserve the items described above may result in a
motion for sanctions including, but not limited to, an adverse
inference instruction, monetary sanctions, costs and fees, and
in serious cases dismissal or default judgment. See VOOM HD
Holdings LLC v. EchoStar Satellite L.L.C., 93 A.D.3d 33
(1st Dep't 2012); Pegasus Aviation I, Inc. v. Varig Logistica
S.A., 26 N.Y.3d 543 (2015).

This letter is sent without prejudice to any rights or remedies,
all of which are expressly reserved.

Sincerely,

_______________________________
Zachary Robert Ancona
