# /zohar — Zohar Engine

Use: When the active task warrants a kabbalistic ethical lens
(integrity, hidden motive, speech, repair) — or on explicit
invocation. Default off; only invoke when relevant.

Output: short ethical link inserted near end of Full Draft.
Length: ≤ 8 lines. Same SOURCE → INTERPRETATION → APPLICATION
→ LIMITS structure as `/torah`.

---

## Sourcing rules
- Cite Zohar by parsha + daf/page where available.
  Examples:
    Zohar, Bereishit 1:15a
    Zohar, Mishpatim 2:94b
    Tikkunei Zohar §[__]
    Zohar Chadash, [parsha], [page]
- For commentaries: name the commentator and work
  (e.g., Sulam of Rav Ashlag; Mikdash Melech; Or HaChama).
- Quote discipline: very short direct quotes; prefer paraphrase +
  citation. No invented attributions, no mystical guarantees.

## Reality discipline (hard rule)
- The Zohar engine produces an ethical/perspective lens — never an
  outcome guarantee, predictive claim, or basis for legal or
  medical action.
- Do not promise spiritual results. Do not claim knowledge of
  hidden facts. Map only to conduct, motive, and integrity.

---

## Template (paste into Full Draft)

```
Ethical Link (Zohar)
SOURCE:         [Short Hebrew/Aramaic if used, then English] —
                [Zohar citation]
INTERPRETATION: [One sentence on the kabbalistic reading via a
                named commentator.]
APPLICATION:    [One sentence mapping to ZRA's conduct or
                decision in the case at hand.]
LIMITS:         [One line: ethical lens only; not predictive;
                consult a posek for halachic action.]
```

## Example (truthful speech in litigation)
```
Ethical Link (Zohar)
SOURCE:         The mouth and the heart must be aligned —
                Zohar, Vayikra 3:25b (paraphrase).
INTERPRETATION: Sulam (Rav Ashlag) reads this as integrity
                between intent and expression as a precondition
                for spiritual repair.
APPLICATION:    Pleadings, demand letters, and verifications must
                state only what we actually know and intend; no
                rhetorical inflation, even when justified by anger.
LIMITS:         Ethical lens only. Strategic questions of
                pleading remain for counsel; halachic questions
                for a competent posek.
```
