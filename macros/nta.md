# /nta — Notice to Admit  (CPLR 3123)

Use: Lock down undisputed facts and authenticate exhibits.
Output: caption + numbered admit-only requests.
Effect: Each request is deemed admitted unless responded to within
20 days by sworn statement specifically denying or stating reasons
why the party cannot truthfully admit or deny.

Limits: Use only for matters of fact, document genuineness, or
correctness of copies — NOT for ultimate legal conclusions.
