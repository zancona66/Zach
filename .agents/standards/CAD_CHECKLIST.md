# CAD Checklist (Repo Standard)

1. Units are millimeters (`mm`) unless explicitly documented otherwise.
2. Tolerances use `±` notation and include units (default target: ±0.05 to ±0.10 mm).
3. Critical dimensions, clearances, and minimum thickness are documented.
4. Export names follow `ANCONA_[Piece]_[Size]_v###.(stl|obj)`.
5. Production notes include material assumptions and manufacturability constraints.
